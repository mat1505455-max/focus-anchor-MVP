Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

The short version

FocusAnchor keeps everything on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, receive or sell any data about you.

What the extension stores

The following is saved in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leaves your device:

  • Your first name or nickname, if you enter one, and your chosen voice.
  • Your list of sites to pause, and your pause-free time setting.
  • A simple local log of events (a pause was shown, the anchor was completed, you continued, or you closed the tab) with a timestamp and the site's domain. It is used only to show your own weekly summary and is limited to the most recent 5,000 events.

The extension does not read the content of pages you visit, your page history, form fields or passwords.

Permissions, and why they are needed

  • Access to specific sites. To show the pause, the extension needs access to the sites on your list. It starts with a short list of common distraction sites and asks for your permission before adding any other site you choose.
  • Storage. To save the settings and local log described above.
  • Scripting. To attach the pause screen to the sites on your list.
  • Web navigation. To notice when a site changes pages without a full reload (single-page apps) so the pause can appear. The addresses are checked on your device against your list and are not stored or transmitted.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data. You can also clear it from your browser's extension settings.

Not medical advice

FocusAnchor is a self-regulation tool. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
