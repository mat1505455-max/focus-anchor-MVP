A 5-second cognitive reset for the scroll.

When you find yourself scrolling on autopilot, FocusAnchor gives your body something real to hold on to, then lets you choose what happens next.

Get FocusAnchor Try the web version

How it works

  1. 1. NoticeYou open a site you tend to lose time on. A calm full-screen pause appears instead of the feed.
  2. 2. AnchorPress and hold for 5 seconds. Touch, sound and a slow-filling ring pull your attention into the present moment.
  3. 3. ChooseTry a tiny grounding step, continue on purpose, or close the tab. It's always your call.

Not a blocker. A reset.

Automatic scrolling and "frozen" moments often feel like the same thing from the inside: your attention is stuck in a loop and willpower doesn't help. FocusAnchor doesn't lock you out or shame you. It interrupts the loop with something physical, then hands the decision back to you.

The grounding steps are simple and low-pressure: find three blue objects, press your feet into the floor, put the phone face down for a minute. Nothing asks you to close your eyes, hold your breath, or revisit anything difficult. Skip any of them.

100% on-device

  • No account, no sign-up, no servers.
  • Your name, settings and pause history are stored only in your browser.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no tracking or analytics.
  • It only runs on a short starter list of sites, plus any you add yourself, each with your explicit permission.
